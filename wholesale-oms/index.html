Wholesale orders, payments and VAT reports

A working backend (Python, FastAPI, MySQL 8.4) that takes wholesale orders, tracks payments and refunds, works out the VAT on every line, and gives you monthly and quarterly reports as Excel or CSV.

How the VAT is decided

The rate is looked up from a table by taxpayer type, product category and the order date, then stored on each order line. If a rate changes later, old orders do not change. Another country is a set of new rows in that table, not new code.

TaxpayerWhatRateValid
General taxpayerGoods (standard)13%from 2026-01-01
General taxpayerReduced-rate goods (e.g. agricultural products, books)9%from 2026-01-01
General taxpayerServices6%from 2026-01-01
General taxpayerExports (zero-rated)0%from 2026-01-01
Small-scale taxpayerLevy rate, all domestic categories3%from 2026-01-01 (applies again after 2027-12-31)
Small-scale taxpayerPreferential rate1%2026-01-01 to 2027-12-31

China VAT preset, per 2026 VAT Law summaries; confirm with your accountant. The business taxpayer type (general or small-scale) is a setting. Amounts round half-up to 0.01 per line; for tax-inclusive prices net = gross / (1 + rate) and VAT = gross - net, so the pieces always add up exactly.

Tests

39 passed, 0 failed in 8.97 s, run on 2026-11-24 against a real MySQL 8.4.13 container (not a stand-in database).

  • Rounding: tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive, half-up, net + VAT always equal to the total
  • Mixed rates in one order; order discount spread exactly over the lines; discounts reduce the taxable amount
  • Zero-rated export; rate effective dates (small-scale 1% on 2027-12-31, 3% on 2028-01-01); old orders keep their rate when a rate changes
  • Order workflow including refused moves; locked lines after confirmation; payment statuses; refunds cannot exceed the payment
  • Report totals equal the sum of the ledger and the order totals; months and quarters add up to the year; CSV and Excel structure
  • Logins, wrong passwords, deactivated users, admin versus staff permissions, audit trail
  • Adding another country's tax rates as data only

Also included

  • Admin and staff roles; admin manages users, tax rates and settings; every change to orders, payments, refunds and rates is written to an audit log with old and new values.
  • Filters and pages on every list (status, customer, date range).
  • Invoice (fapiao) number on orders and a credit-note reference on refunds, as plain fields.
  • Setup and test instructions in the README (inside the zip). A Docker Compose file for MySQL and the app is included as a starting point; the run on this page used MySQL in Docker and the app in a Python environment.

Not covered, honestly

  • No link to the Golden Tax system or e-invoicing: invoice numbers are just stored.
  • Output VAT only: no input VAT deduction, withholding or local surcharges.
  • The rates are a starting preset, not tax advice.
  • One currency per order, no exchange-rate conversion in the reports.
