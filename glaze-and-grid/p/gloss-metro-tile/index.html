About this tile

Glossy bevelled wall tiles in two lengths, 7.5 × 15 and 7.5 × 30 cm, in white, sage or ink blue. The classic for kitchens and bathrooms: for walls, splashbacks and wet rooms. Lay them straight, in brick bond or in herringbone.

How much do you need?

Whole boxes with the waste your pattern needs, then the kit to fix them. Coverage rules are sample data.

Example with the sample rules: a 2 × 1 m wall (2 m²) laid straight needs 5 boxes: 2.20 m² with 10% waste, 2.48 m² ordered, €71.55 for the tiles at home prices. One shade lot covers it (2620B). With adhesive, grout and the rest of the kit: €106.35.
