About this tile

Matt porcelain with the calm, cloudy surface of poured concrete. Three greys (light, mid and dark) in three sizes: 30 × 60, 60 × 60 and 60 × 120 cm, all rectified. For floors, walls, wet rooms and outdoor areas, and the two rectangles can also go in herringbone.

How much do you need?

Whole boxes with the waste your pattern needs, then the kit to fix them. Coverage rules are sample data.

Example with the sample rules: a 4 × 3 m floor (12 m²) laid straight needs 10 boxes: 13.20 m² with 10% waste, 14.40 m² ordered, €324.00 for the tiles at home prices. One shade lot covers it (2628B). With adhesive, grout and the rest of the kit: €446.20.
