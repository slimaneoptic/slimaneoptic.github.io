About this tile

Porcelain hexagons, 15 cm across the flats, with a satin glaze. Four colours (bone, terracotta, fog and graphite), 26 tiles per box, for floors, walls and wet rooms. Sample slip rating R10.

How much do you need?

Whole boxes with the waste your pattern needs, then the kit to fix them. Coverage rules are sample data.

Example with the sample rules: a 4 × 3 m floor (12 m²) laid straight needs 27 boxes: 13.20 m² with 10% waste, 13.69 m² ordered, €551.61 for the tiles at home prices. One shade lot covers it (2522C). With adhesive, grout and the rest of the kit: €621.21.
