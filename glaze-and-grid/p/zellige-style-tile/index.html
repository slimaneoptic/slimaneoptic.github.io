About this tile

Zellige-style square tiles, 10 × 10 cm, in clay glazed by hand in a Fez workshop. Six glazes (chalk white, cobalt, bottle green, ochre, dusty rose and charcoal), and each tile varies in tone and edge, by design. For walls and splashbacks, 50 tiles (0.5 m²) per box, with a supplier lead time of 2 to 4 weeks by glaze.

How much do you need?

Whole boxes with the waste your pattern needs, then the kit to fix them. Coverage rules are sample data.

Example with the sample rules: a 2 × 1 m wall (2 m²) laid straight needs 5 boxes: 2.30 m² with 15% waste, 2.50 m² ordered, €284.75 for the tiles at home prices. One shade lot covers it (F-119). With adhesive, grout and the rest of the kit: €319.55.
