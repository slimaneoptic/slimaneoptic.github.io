About this tile

Porcelain planks with the grain of oak, in natural, smoked or bleached. Two sizes, 20 × 120 and 20 × 180 cm, both matt and 1.44 m² per box. Lay them straight, in brick bond or in herringbone, on floors, walls and wet rooms.

How much do you need?

Whole boxes with the waste your pattern needs, then the kit to fix them. Coverage rules are sample data.

Example with the sample rules: a 4 × 3 m floor (12 m²) laid straight needs 10 boxes: 13.44 m² with 12% waste, 14.40 m² ordered, €445.00 for the tiles at home prices. One shade lot covers it (2609A). With adhesive, grout and the rest of the kit: €587.10.
