Trade terms

This is a demo policy. Glaze & Grid is a fictional shop, and so is its trade counter. No accounts are opened and no orders are taken. Terms marked "sample" are made up for the demo.

Who it's for

Tilers, builders, renovation firms and interior designers who buy tiles for their work.

Trade prices

  • One trade price per item, at any quantity. It's about 18% below the price for 1 to 9 boxes, and below the full-pallet price too.
  • It covers the tiles and the kit: adhesive, grout, trims and levelling clips.
  • Sample boxes are free for trade accounts.

Paying on account

  • Invoices are due 30 days after the invoice date (sample).
  • We agree a credit limit when the account opens (sample).
  • If an invoice is overdue, new orders wait until it's paid (sample).

Ordering

  • Order by SKU: type or paste a list of SKUs and box counts.
  • Ask for a quote for a job. It holds its prices for 30 days (sample) and turns into an order in one step.
  • Reorder from the same shade lot as your last job, while that lot has stock.

Delivery and returns

We deliver to your site or yard. The delivery and returns pages apply to trade orders too, and refunds go back to your account as a credit.

Opening an account

A real shop would ask for your business name, address and company registration here. In this demo there is nothing to apply for.

In this demo

Press "Trade buyer" at the top of the page ("Trade" on a phone) to see trade prices. A trade login, order by SKU, quotes, pay on account and reorders come with the WooCommerce version of this store (phase 2).
