How it works

  • pdf.js renders the page and locates the text run under your click (text content + operator list).
  • The word's glyphs are replaced in the content stream; Tc, Tw and Tz are honoured and widths recomputed from the font's widths.
  • Arabic is shaped (HarfBuzz WASM, joining forms, lam-alef) with bidi for digits and English inside Arabic; right-to-left lines keep their right edge.
  • Order of preference: the embedded font itself, then glyphs already in its subset, then a bundled fallback (DejaVu Sans, Arabic and Latin) embedded as a subset.
  • Every new glyph gets its own ToUnicode text, so the new word extracts, searches and copies correctly. Fonts that draw dots or hamza as separate glyphs cannot map them cleanly, so only the edited word switches to the fallback font. The file is saved with pdf-lib.
Fonts: DejaVu Sans (DejaVu license). Libraries: pdf.js, pdf-lib, fontkit, HarfBuzz, bidi-js.
