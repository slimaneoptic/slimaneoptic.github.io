Safari

Safari limits cookies written by JavaScript to 7 days and clears script-written storage after 7 days without a visit. So on Safari the 30 to 90 days only holds if the visitor returns within 7 days. The usual fix is a tiny uncached endpoint on your site that re-sends the same cookie from the server; page caching does not affect it.
