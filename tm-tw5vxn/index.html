How it works

  1. 1Type your room. Its width and depth, then the doors and windows. The drawing builds as you type.
  2. 2Pick the furniture you want. Typical sizes, or your own.
  3. 3Get three layouts that fit, each with its proof: every gap measured, every path walked, every door swung.

What we check

Checked, not guessed. One part plans the furniture; separate code checks every layout in exact centimetres, and a layout it rejects is never shown.

Fit, never bent

  • Every piece inside the walls, with no overlaps.
  • Every door swings free.
  • A path from the door you come in by: at least 60 cm wide to every other door, 45 cm to every piece you use.
  • 60 cm of floor to step in at every door.

Comfort, default rules you can change

  • Main paths 80 cm wide, other paths 50 cm.
  • Room to use each piece: beside the bed, in front of the wardrobe and the desk.
  • Every window can be reached, and nothing taller than its sill stands within 30 cm of it.
  • Furniture covers at most 35 % of the floor.

What we don't check

Every layout says so too, under "Not checked".

  • Whether your numbers are right.
  • Getting pieces in through doors, halls and stairs.
  • Skirting boards, sockets, switches and thermostats.
  • Sloped ceilings, beams and angled walls.
  • Windows that open inward, unless you marked them.
  • Wall-mounted things, rugs, lamps, curtains and cables.
  • Style.
  • The AI picture.
