Slimane ChanchoulDemo: video strip

A smooth strip of portrait videos, light to load

Sample clips made for this demo. They are plain colour animations, not real footage.

What you see

A continuous strip of 9:16 videos. They are muted, loop with no jump, and play inline on phones. There is no horizontal scrollbar at phone width.

How it loads: each clip shows a poster image first. A clip only loads and plays while it is on screen, and it pauses when it leaves. Sizes are fixed, so the page does not shift. With reduced motion turned on, the strip stands still.

Swapping videos later

In this demo the clip list is one small list at the top of the script. On the WordPress site, the same list would come from an Elementor widget where the owner picks videos from the Media Library. That is the plan. It is not built here.
