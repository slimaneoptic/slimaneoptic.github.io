Person mode finds people on its own.

Honest note. Real Portrait Mode uses the iPhone's depth data, which a web page cannot read; this test uses an AI cutout, which works best on people.

What the page does

  1. Opens the iPhone's own camera (full resolution, Apple autofocus and exposure). No custom camera.
  2. Finds the subject with an on-device AI model (MediaPipe, CPU) and snaps the cutout edges to the photo.
  3. No person in the photo (a product, a cup, a shoe)? It keeps the sharpest object on its own; tap another one to change it.
  4. Blurs the background more the farther it is from the subject, without a glow around the edges.
  5. Lifts the light (levels and exposure) and lightly sharpens the subject.
