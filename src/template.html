Pipeline operations scheduler

Operators planned an industrial pipeline network by hand, a week ahead: which feeders run, where the flow goes, when the pipeline pumps or stops, with 12 storage tanks kept within safe limits and clients served around the clock.

I built a constraint-programming model of the whole network, about 10,800 decisions and 15,000 operating rules per run, with the pipeline's 25-hour transit delay built in. A simulator validates every schedule before it is used, projection alerts fire before a limit is hit, and the planning systems request new schedules through an API. In production for six months.
