A plain third-party page

This page uses none of the styles of slimanechanchoul.com. It adds the assistant the way a client would, with one line before </body>:

<script src="https://slimanechanchoul.com/widget.js" data-site="docs.python.org/3/tutorial/" data-color="#1F64B0" defer></script>

Open the bubble at the bottom right. On the first open, the assistant reads the site in data-site (public pages only, about 20 seconds), then answers from it with sources. The widget keeps its own styles in a Shadow DOM, so this page's serif font and colors do not leak in.
